How to Play

Each batch is 5 guesses. Every guess gives you a name and two stat categories — say, Points/Game vs. Rebounds/Game.

Press and drag on the grid to place where you think that entry's real numbers land: left-right for the first stat, bottom-top for the second. Release to lock it in.

Your score is based on how close your guess lands to the real point — up to 1000 per guess, 5000 per batch.

Toggle multiple packs on at once (NBA, Movies, Countries, ...) to mix them — each guess in the batch randomly draws from whichever packs are enabled.

Once you finish a batch, copy your results to share with friends.
